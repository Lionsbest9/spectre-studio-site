ENT / ORLear, nose and throat

A consultation unit for the ear, nose and throat with these examinations:

  • Otoscopy
  • Rhinoscopy
  • Flexible laryngoscopy
  • Flexible nasofibroscopy

Equipment named on the site: an ear irrigation tool (syringing and removal of foreign bodies), a suction machine for nose, ear and throat, and a warming tool for secretions.

Source: the clinic's ENT/ORL service page.
